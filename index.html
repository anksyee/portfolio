01 — cartography · 2026

this is a test

test on modelling art field

The positions are objects — artist, gallery, critic, public, collector — and the artwork is the arrow between them. A category must compose: a work goes artist→gallery, another gallery→critic, so the axioms demand a third, artist→critic, whether or not any work made that move.

Force every such composite and 7 relations become 15. Eight are fabricated: realised by no artwork, demanded by the rule. The model works — and that is the problem. A clean fit is a proof of what had to be forced, not an orderly field.

Developed under the guidance of Edgar Venezuela, PhD, who introduced the categorical reading of the field and supervised the derivation.

02 — simulation · 2026

the slop engine

post · reshare · opening · void

Now the field opens. The work stops terminating at the public — there is the opening, the Instagram post, the reshares of the post, the resale. Work keeps circulating. Four views of one work, and no two agree on title, price or provenance.

A sheaf wants views that match on overlaps and glue into one. These don't. Add a view and resolution falls: a presheaf that will not glue. Visibility becomes more circulation, not an end — so the network adds a terminal of its own: the void, where the over-circulated work empties out.

03 — derivation · 2026

a test cartography

closure, computed

The same field, set down as computation. cl(R) = R ∪ R² ∪ R³ ∪ R⁴ ∪ R⁵. Two-step routes close six pairs the field never marks directly; three steps close the last two. Longer routes only revisit what is already registered — the height is 5.

Seven given, fifteen forced. Because every forward pair is present the order is total, not merely partial: artist below all, public above all. A thin poset with an initial and a terminal and nothing that looks both ways — no centre, surviving the forcing.

05 — paper & playable space · 2026

liminality mechanisms

the fold, the grid, and the void

Transit cuts through striated space to open a temporary pocket of what Deleuze and Guattari call smooth space. For a certain amount of time, between point A and point B, you are off the grid.

The initial intuition drags us toward the linear: you enter at A, travel a corridor, exit at B as a slightly altered person. But B does not exist. There was only ever A, and B is just the other side of A. You can't see who you are becoming while you become it — identity is strictly retroactive.

Classic anthropology treated the in-between as a problem to be escaped. But coming into a stable position is also an enclosure: to settle into one state is to foreclose the others. To choose permanent liminality is to live an unscripted existence as something cathartic rather than a transitional crisis.

LIMINAL EXPRESS is the practice to that paper. You board and walk — the subway, the void, the backrooms, an endless suburbia — with no objective and no destination, looping. Walking these spaces aimlessly is the simulation of permanent liminality: the transition that never completes.

06 — imaging & sonification · 2026

did you know that mice watch the matrix?

two-photon imaging of a mouse visual cortex, played back as music

A mouse watches thirty-second movie clips — ten of them, over and over, for eighteen minutes — while a two-photon microscope records the calcium activity of individual neurons in its visual cortex. Every bright cell is a neuron firing.

The recording is also a score. Each detected event in each cell becomes a note: the cell's position on the sensor sets its pitch, its trace sets the rhythm. Nothing is composed — the music is what the brain was already doing while it watched.
