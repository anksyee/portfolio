02 · the claim

this field has no centre. testing the model to see how it develops

03 · the objects · N = 6

the positions are objects: artist, gallery, collector, museum, critic, public. the artwork is the arrows() between the objects, traveling through the filed

|E| = 7 · all verified

04 · the forcing · axioms

a category must compose. a work goes artist→gallery, another gallery→collector — so the axioms demand a third, artist→collector, whether or not any work made that move. force every such composite and 7 relations become 15. eight are fabricated: realised by no artwork, demanded by the rule.

|E| = 7 → 15 · 8 forced

05 · the ladder · total order

closure ⟶ total order. artist = initial (bottom), public = terminal (top). every pair comparable; nothing parallel. a field with no hierarchy, rendered as pure hierarchy.

artist = initial · public = terminal

06 · no centre · zero object

no zero object

∅ 0 · initial ≠ terminal

07 · the twist

here's the twist: the model works. and that's the problem. It only works by flattening a field that has no hierarchy and no centre into one that does. so a clean fit is a proof of what had to be forced, rather than an orderly filed.

clean fit = proof of forcing

08 · artist as a product

artist = curator × manager × dealer × archivist

The model treats the artist as one clean object. It isn't. The artist is curator, manager, dealer, and archivist at once — every role the field pays someone else to do, done by one exhausted node. Categorically that's a product: a single position these four roles factor through. Even the objects aren't atomic. The striation fails here too.

09 · the field opens

now we leave the closed six-position model and enter the networked one. the work doesn't stop at the public anymore, there's the opening, the Instagram post, the reshares of the post, the resale. work keeps circulating. the field opens

opening · post · reshare · resale

10 · saturation · presheaf

post · reshare · opening · resale — four views of one work. sheaf = views that match on overlaps, glued into one. these don't match. add a view ⟶ less resolution. a presheaf that will not glue.

views: 0 → 30 · conflicts: 0 → 46 · totalisable: NO

11 · the void

market: terminates at public. network: runs past it. visibility ⟶ more circulation, not an end. so the network adds a new terminal — the void — where the over-circulated work empties out. art ends not in its audience but in the nothing after it.

∅ · the void
